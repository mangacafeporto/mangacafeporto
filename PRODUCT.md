# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors discovering Manga Café Porto — manga fans, remote workers, students, and creative community members in Porto, Portugal. Most arrive via mobile (searching for cafés nearby), some via social media links (Instagram, Facebook). They want to quickly understand what this place is, how much it costs, and whether it's worth visiting.

Secondary audience: existing community members checking hours, events, or sharing the site with friends.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Manga Café Porto, a manga café and creative community space at R. de Cedofeita 170, Porto. The site communicates the café's identity, pricing, offerings, and location. Success means a visitor understands what the space is within 3 seconds and feels compelled to visit.

## Brand Personality

Cozy, handmade, neighborhood-scale.

- **Voice:** Warm and welcoming, like a friend inviting you over. Not corporate, not try-hard cool.
- **Tone:** Quiet confidence. The space speaks for itself.
- **Emotional target:** Within 3 seconds of landing, the visitor should feel cozy, warm, and welcomed — like stepping into a friend's living room full of manga.

Three words: **warm**, **indie**, **handmade**.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate SaaS patterns:** 3-column feature grids with icons, testimonial carousels, "Trusted by" logo strips, Inter/Helvetica body text.
- **Old-web kitsch:** GIFs, marquees, visitor counters, blinking text, table-layout borders, Geocities chaos.
- **Stock Japanese motifs:** Cherry blossoms, torii gates, Mt. Fuji silhouettes, rising sun rays, koi fish. The Japanese-ness comes from typography pairing, red/cream/blue palette, and kanji labels — not stock clip art.
- **Cold modernism:** Sterile white backgrounds, minimal-to-the-point-of-anonymous. Everything should feel hand-touched.
- **"Tokyo cool":** Slick, trendy, nightlife aesthetic. This is closer to a beloved local izakaya or a thoughtfully made zine.

## Design Principles

1. **Made with care, not with templates.** Every element should feel hand-placed. Asymmetry and imperfection are features, not bugs.
2. **Show the space, don't sell it.** The café's warmth comes through in typography, color, and layout — not marketing copy.
3. **Retro warmth, modern clarity.** The old-Japanese-web charm lives in type, color, and borders — not in actual old-web techniques.
4. **Breathe.** Generous cream space. Resist the urge to fill every pixel. Restraint is the hardest design choice.
5. **Quiet confidence.** No flashy animations, no parallax, no performative interactivity. The site should feel calm and considered.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance
- Keyboard navigable
- Screen reader compatible (semantic HTML, ARIA labels)
- Reduced motion support (respect `prefers-reduced-motion`)
- Color contrast: ensure crimson (#C8302C) and cobalt (#1E47B8) meet AA ratios against cream (#F5EDE0)
- Bilingual: Portuguese (default) and English
